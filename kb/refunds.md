# Refund & Cancellation Policy

## Standard Refund Eligibility Window
All customers are eligible for a **full refund within 14 days** of their initial purchase. Refund eligibility is based on the original transaction date. This applies to Pro, Business, and Enterprise plans equally.

## Enterprise Satisfaction Guarantee Window
Enterprise-tier customers receive an extended **30-day "Satisfaction Guarantee"** window for a full refund, starting from the initial purchase date. This is in addition to the standard 14-day window — Enterprise customers effectively have 30 days to evaluate the platform.

## Automated Refund Processing Cutoff
Loomo's billing system (Stripe) is configured to automatically process refund requests initiated within **14 days** of the original transaction. Refund requests initiated **after 14 days** will be automatically rejected by Stripe and must be processed manually by the billing team. Enterprise customers within their 30-day Satisfaction Guarantee window who are past the 14-day Stripe cutoff should contact billing@loomo.ai for manual processing.

## No Prorated Refunds for Mid-Cycle Cancellations
Loomo does **not** offer prorated refunds when a customer cancels mid-billing cycle. When you cancel, your service remains fully active through the end of your current billing period. This applies to all plan tiers.

## Cancellation Process
Cancellations can be initiated by the Account Owner in **Settings → Billing → Cancel Subscription**. Upon cancellation:
- Service remains active through the end of the current billing period
- No further invoices are generated
- Data is retained according to the plan's data retention period (see Data Privacy policy)
- The account is not deleted — it transitions to a "cancelled" state with read-only access after the billing period ends

## Refund Approval Thresholds
Refund amounts **below $2,500** can be approved and processed by **Senior Support Leads**. Any refund request **exceeding $2,500** requires written approval from the **VP of Finance** before processing. This threshold applies to individual refund transactions, not aggregate amounts.

## Refund Processing Time
Approved refunds are processed within **5-7 business days**. Refunds are issued to the original payment method. Credit card refunds typically appear within 5-10 business days depending on the card issuer. ACH refunds may take up to 10 business days.

## Annual Contract Cancellations
Customers on annual billing contracts who wish to cancel before the contract term expires are subject to an **early termination fee** equal to the lesser of: (a) 3 months of subscription fees, or (b) the remaining balance of the contract. Enterprise customers with negotiated terms should refer to their specific agreement.
