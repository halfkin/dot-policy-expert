# Refund & Cancellation Policy

## Standard Refund Eligibility Window
Customers are eligible for a full refund within **14 days** of their initial purchase. Refund eligibility is based on the transaction date of the initial purchase.

## Enterprise Satisfaction Guarantee Window
Enterprise-tier customers have a **30-day “Satisfaction Guarantee”** window for a full refund. This window starts from the initial purchase date.

## Automated Refund Processing Cutoff
The automated Stripe billing system is hard-coded to reject refund requests initiated after **14 days** from the initial transaction date. Refund requests initiated after this cutoff will be rejected by the system.

## No Prorated Refunds for Mid-Cycle Cancellations
Loomo does **not** offer prorated refunds for mid-cycle cancellations. Services remain active until the end of the current billing period.

## Refund Approval Thresholds
Any refund request exceeding **$2,500** requires written approval from the **VP of Finance**. Refund amounts **below $2,500** are handled by **Senior Support Leads**.

## Cancellation Effect on Service Access
When a customer cancels mid-cycle, service remains active through the end of the current billing period. Cancellation does not trigger a prorated refund.
