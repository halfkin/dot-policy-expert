# Account Management Policy

## Plan Pricing by Tier
**Pro** is priced at **$49/user/month** with a **5-seat minimum**. **Business** is **$92/user/month** with a **15-seat minimum**, and **Enterprise** is **$165/user/month** with a **50-seat minimum**.

## Plan Upgrades
Upgrades take effect **immediately**. The price difference is charged at the time of the upgrade.

## Plan Downgrades
Downgrades take effect at the start of the **next billing cycle**. The current plan remains active until the new cycle begins.

## Account Ownership Transfer via Admin Dashboard
Account ownership can be transferred via the **Admin Dashboard**. This is the standard method for transferring ownership.

## Ownership Transfer When Current Owner Is Unreachable
If the current owner is unreachable, a **notarized letter of authority** is required to transfer ownership. This requirement applies specifically to unreachable-owner cases.

## Account Deletion Soft-Delete Period
Accounts marked for deletion enter a “soft-delete” state for **45 days**. Permanent data scrubbing occurs after the soft-delete period ends.
