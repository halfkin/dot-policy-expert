# Account Management

## Plan Tiers and Pricing
Loomo offers three plans:
- **Pro**: $49 per user per month with a **5-seat minimum** ($245/month minimum)
- **Business**: $92 per user per month with a **15-seat minimum** ($1,380/month minimum)
- **Enterprise**: $165 per user per month with a **50-seat minimum** ($8,250/month minimum)

All plans are billed monthly by default. Annual billing is available for Business and Enterprise customers at a **15% discount**.

## Plan Upgrades
Upgrades take effect **immediately**. The price difference is prorated for the remaining billing cycle and charged at the time of upgrade. All features of the new plan are available as soon as the upgrade is processed.

## Plan Downgrades
Downgrades take effect at the start of the **next billing cycle**. The current plan's features remain active until the new cycle begins. No prorated credit is issued for unused time on the higher plan. Any features exclusive to the higher plan (e.g., API access, SSO) will become unavailable when the downgrade activates.

## Minimum Seat Requirements
Each plan has a minimum seat count: Pro (5), Business (15), Enterprise (50). Downgrading below the minimum seat count of a plan tier requires moving to a lower tier. You cannot have a Business plan with fewer than 15 seats.

## Account Ownership and Admin Roles
Each Loomo account has exactly **one Owner** with full administrative privileges. The Owner can designate **Admins** who can manage users, billing, and settings. **Members** have access to platform features but cannot modify account settings.

## Transferring Account Ownership
The current Owner can transfer ownership via the **Admin Dashboard** under **Settings → Account → Transfer Ownership**. The new owner must be an existing user on the account. Ownership transfer is immediate and cannot be undone without the new owner's consent.

## Ownership Transfer When Owner Is Unreachable
If the current account owner has left the organization and cannot be contacted, the requesting party must submit a **notarized letter of authority** to support@loomo.ai. The letter must be on company letterhead and signed by an authorized officer. Loomo will process the transfer within **10 business days** of receiving the letter.

## Account Deletion Process
Account deletion can be initiated by the Owner in **Settings → Account → Delete Account**. Upon initiation, the account enters a **45-day soft-delete period** during which:
- All users lose access immediately
- Data is preserved but inaccessible
- The deletion can be reversed by contacting support@loomo.ai

After the 45-day period, all data is **permanently scrubbed** and cannot be recovered.

## Account Reactivation
Accounts in the soft-delete period (first 45 days after deletion) can be reactivated by the original Owner contacting support@loomo.ai. Reactivation restores all data, users, and settings. Accounts cannot be reactivated after the 45-day period ends.

## Multi-Account Management
Organizations with multiple Loomo accounts (e.g., separate departments) can link them under a single billing entity by contacting sales@loomo.ai. Linked accounts maintain separate data and user lists but share a consolidated invoice.
