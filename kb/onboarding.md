# Customer Onboarding Guide

## Pro Plan Self-Serve Onboarding
Pro plan onboarding is self-serve through **Loomo Academy** video tutorials. Loomo Academy covers onboarding via guided video content.

## Business Plan Onboarding Sessions
Business plan customers receive two **60-minute** onboarding sessions with a dedicated **Onboarding Specialist (OS)**. Target completion is **14 days**.

## Enterprise Implementation Plan Timeline
Enterprise customers receive a custom implementation plan managed by a dedicated **Customer Success Manager (CSM)**. Target completion is **30 days**.

## Dedicated CSM Assignment Threshold
Any account with **ARR over $15,000** is automatically assigned a **Dedicated CSM**, regardless of plan tier. This assignment is based on the ARR threshold.

## Support Channels for Pro Plan
Pro plan support is provided via **email** with a **24-hour** response target. Support is delivered through the email channel for Pro customers.

## Support Channels for Business Plan
Business plan support includes **Priority Email + Chat**. Business customers can use both channels under this support model.

## Support Channels for Enterprise Plan
Enterprise plan support includes a **Dedicated CSM** and **24/7 phone** support. Enterprise customers have access to phone support around the clock.
