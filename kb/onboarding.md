# Onboarding & Support

## Pro Plan Onboarding
Pro plan onboarding is **self-serve** through **Loomo Academy** — a library of video tutorials, quickstart guides, and interactive walkthroughs. Loomo Academy covers account setup, user management, core features, and best practices. Most Pro customers are fully onboarded within **7 days**.

## Business Plan Onboarding
Business plan customers receive **two 60-minute onboarding sessions** with a dedicated **Onboarding Specialist (OS)**. Sessions cover account configuration, user provisioning, workflow setup, and integration planning. Target onboarding completion is **14 days** from purchase. Business customers also have full access to Loomo Academy for self-serve learning.

## Enterprise Plan Onboarding
Enterprise customers receive a **custom implementation plan** managed by a dedicated **Customer Success Manager (CSM)**. The implementation plan includes: discovery and scoping, technical integration, data migration assistance, user training, and go-live support. Target onboarding completion is **30 days** from contract signing, though complex implementations may take up to 60 days.

## Dedicated Customer Success Manager
Any account with **Annual Recurring Revenue (ARR) over $15,000** is automatically assigned a dedicated CSM, regardless of plan tier. CSMs provide ongoing strategic guidance, quarterly business reviews, and proactive support. CSM assignments are communicated via email within **5 business days** of qualification.

## Support Channels by Plan

### Pro Plan Support
- **Email support** with a **24-hour** response target (business days)
- Access to **Loomo Community Forum** for peer-to-peer assistance
- Access to **Loomo Academy** self-serve resources
- **No phone support** or live chat on Pro plan

### Business Plan Support
- **Priority email** with a **12-hour** response target (business days)
- **Live chat** available Monday–Friday, **8 AM – 6 PM CST**
- Access to Loomo Community Forum and Loomo Academy
- **No phone support** on Business plan

### Enterprise Plan Support
- **Dedicated CSM** as primary point of contact
- **24/7 phone support** for P1 (critical) issues
- **Priority email** with a **4-hour** response target (all days)
- **Live chat** available 24/7
- Access to all self-serve resources

## Support Priority Levels
Support requests are prioritized based on severity:
- **P1 (Critical)**: Platform completely unavailable or data loss — response within **1 hour** (Enterprise) or **4 hours** (Business/Pro)
- **P2 (High)**: Major feature broken with no workaround — response within **4 hours** (Enterprise) or **12 hours** (Business/Pro)
- **P3 (Medium)**: Feature issue with a workaround available — response within **1 business day**
- **P4 (Low)**: General question, feature request, or minor issue — response within **2 business days**

## Loomo Academy
Loomo Academy is a free resource available to all customers and includes:
- **Getting Started** series (5 videos, ~45 minutes total)
- **Admin Configuration** guide
- **Integration Tutorials** for supported platforms
- **Best Practices** library updated quarterly
- **Release Notes** with feature walkthroughs for each update

Access Loomo Academy at **academy.loomo.ai** or through the in-app help menu.
