# Acceptable Use Policy

## Prohibited Data Scraping
Customers may not scrape Loomo’s proprietary database. This prohibition applies to any attempt to extract Loomo proprietary data via automated means.

## Prohibited Adult Content Hosting
Customers may not host **adult content** using Loomo. Any adult content hosting is considered a prohibited use.

## Shared Login Prohibition
“Shared logins” are prohibited: **one human per seat**. Each seat must be assigned to a single individual user.

## Enforcement for Violations
First-time violations result in a **warning**. Second-time violations result in a **7-day suspension**, and third-time violations result in a **permanent ban without a refund**.

## Appeals Process and Deadline
Appeals must be submitted to **trust@loomo.ai** within **48 hours** of the enforcement action. Appeals submitted after 48 hours do not meet the stated deadline.
