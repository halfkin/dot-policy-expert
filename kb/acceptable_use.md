# Acceptable Use Policy

## Prohibited Uses
The following activities are prohibited on the Loomo platform:
- **Data scraping**: Automated extraction of Loomo's proprietary data, databases, or content
- **Adult content**: Hosting, distributing, or linking to adult or sexually explicit material
- **Illegal activity**: Using Loomo to facilitate any activity that violates applicable law
- **Malware distribution**: Uploading or distributing malicious software, viruses, or harmful code
- **Abuse of platform resources**: Cryptocurrency mining, excessive API abuse, or denial-of-service attacks against Loomo infrastructure

## Shared Login Prohibition
Shared logins are strictly prohibited. Each Loomo seat must be assigned to **one individual human user**. Sharing credentials between team members, using generic/shared accounts (e.g., team@company.com), or using automated tools to share a single session across multiple users are all violations.

## API Usage Limits
API access is governed by per-plan rate limits:
- **Pro**: 1,000 API calls per hour
- **Business**: 5,000 API calls per hour
- **Enterprise**: 25,000 API calls per hour (higher limits available by request)

Sustained usage above plan limits will result in throttling. Repeated or intentional abuse may result in enforcement action.

## Content and Data Responsibilities
Customers are solely responsible for the content they upload, store, and distribute through Loomo. Loomo does not actively monitor customer content but will act on reports of policy violations. Customers must ensure their use of Loomo complies with all applicable data protection laws in their jurisdiction.

## Enforcement Actions for Violations
Loomo uses a progressive enforcement model:
- **First violation**: Written **warning** via email to the account Owner and billing contact
- **Second violation**: **7-day account suspension** with restricted access to all platform features
- **Third violation**: **Permanent account ban** without refund of any prepaid fees

Loomo reserves the right to skip steps in this progression for severe violations (e.g., illegal activity, active harm to other users, or security threats).

## Appeals Process
Appeals must be submitted to **trust@loomo.ai** within **48 hours** of the enforcement action. Appeals must include: the ticket or reference number of the enforcement notice, a description of the circumstances, and any supporting documentation. Loomo will review and respond to appeals within **5 business days**. The decision on appeal is final.

## Reporting Violations
Users can report suspected policy violations by other customers by emailing **trust@loomo.ai** with details of the violation. Reports are reviewed within **3 business days**. Reporters are notified of the outcome unless disclosure would compromise an investigation.
