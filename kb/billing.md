# Billing & Payments Policy

## Accepted Payment Methods
Loomo accepts **Credit Card (Stripe)** payments. **ACH** is available for **Business** and **Enterprise** customers, and **paper checks are not accepted**.

## Monthly Invoice Generation
Invoices are generated on the **1st of every month**. This applies to Loomo’s standard invoicing cycle.

## Late Fee Policy
A **1.5% late fee** is applied to balances outstanding after **15 days**. Late fees apply once the balance crosses the 15-day overdue threshold.

## Payment Overdue Suspension Threshold
Accounts are suspended if payment is **30 days overdue**. Suspension occurs based on the overdue timeline.

## Upgrade Billing Treatment
Plan upgrades are effective immediately. The price difference is charged at the time of the upgrade.

## Downgrade Billing Treatment
Plan downgrades only take effect at the start of the next billing cycle. Charges remain at the current plan level until the new cycle begins.

## Billing Currency
All billing is processed in **USD**. Prices and invoices are issued in USD.
