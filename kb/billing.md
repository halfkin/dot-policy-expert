# Billing & Payments

## Accepted Payment Methods
Loomo accepts **Credit Card** payments processed through **Stripe**. **ACH bank transfer** is available for Business and Enterprise customers. **Wire transfer** is available for Enterprise customers with annual contracts. Paper checks are **not accepted**.

## Monthly Invoicing Cycle
Invoices are generated on the **1st of every month** and sent to the billing contact on file. Invoices include an itemized breakdown of seats, plan tier, and any mid-cycle changes. Invoices are available for download in the Admin Dashboard under **Settings → Billing → Invoice History**.

## Net Payment Terms
All invoices are due upon receipt unless otherwise negotiated. Enterprise customers with annual contracts may negotiate **Net-30** or **Net-60** payment terms as part of their agreement.

## Late Payment Policy
A **1.5% monthly late fee** is applied to balances outstanding for more than **15 days** past the invoice date. This fee compounds monthly on the unpaid balance. Automated payment reminders are sent at 7 days, 14 days, and 21 days overdue.

## Account Suspension for Non-Payment
Accounts with balances overdue by **30 days or more** are automatically suspended. Suspended accounts lose access to all platform features, but data is retained. Access is restored within **4 hours** of payment being received and confirmed.

## Billing Currency
All pricing, invoices, and payments are processed in **US Dollars (USD)**. Customers paying by credit card from outside the US may incur foreign transaction fees from their card issuer — Loomo does not control or charge these fees.

## Tax Handling
Loomo does **not** charge sales tax on SaaS subscriptions in most jurisdictions. Where required by law (e.g., certain US states, EU VAT for non-business customers), applicable tax is itemized separately on the invoice. Customers with valid tax-exempt certificates can upload them in **Settings → Billing → Tax Exemption** to avoid future charges.

## Billing Disputes
Billing disputes must be raised within **60 days** of the invoice date by contacting billing@loomo.ai. Disputes raised after 60 days are not eligible for adjustment. Loomo will respond to billing disputes within **5 business days**.

## Upgrade Billing Treatment
Plan upgrades take effect **immediately**. The price difference between the old and new plan is prorated for the remaining days in the current billing cycle and charged at the time of upgrade.

## Downgrade Billing Treatment
Plan downgrades take effect at the start of the **next billing cycle**. The current plan and its features remain active until the cycle ends. No prorated credit is issued for the remaining time on the higher plan.

## Seat Changes Mid-Cycle
Adding seats mid-cycle is prorated — you pay only for the remaining days in the current billing period. Removing seats takes effect at the start of the **next billing cycle**. Removed seats remain active and billable through the end of the current period.
