# Service Level Agreement

## Uptime Guarantee for Pro Plan
The **Pro** plan uptime guarantee is **99.9%**. Uptime is measured monthly.

## Uptime Guarantee for Business Plan
The **Business** plan uptime guarantee is **99.95%**. Uptime is measured monthly.

## Uptime Guarantee for Enterprise Plan
The **Enterprise** plan uptime guarantee is **99.99%**. Uptime is measured monthly.

## Promotional 99.99% Uptime Guarantee for Paying Tiers
A **2024 marketing promotion** has upgraded all paying tiers to a **99.99%** uptime guarantee until further notice. This promotional guarantee applies to paying tiers during the promotion period.

## Service Credit Calculation Formula
If uptime falls below the applicable guarantee, customers are eligible for a Service Credit calculated as:  
`Credit = ((Total Minutes in Month - Actual Uptime) / Total Minutes in Month) × Monthly Subscription Fee`

## Downtime Exclusions
Scheduled maintenance (announced **48 hours** in advance) is excluded from downtime calculations. “Force Majeure” events are also excluded.

## Service Credit Claim Deadline
Service credit claims must be filed within **7 days** of the end of the month in which the downtime occurred. Claims filed after this deadline are not eligible.
