# Employee Time Off Policy (Internal)

## PTO Accrual for 0–2 Years of Tenure
Employees with **0–2 years** of tenure accrue **1.25 days per month**, totaling **15 days per year**. Accrual follows this monthly rate for this tenure band.

## PTO Accrual for 2+ Years of Tenure
Employees with **2+ years** of tenure accrue **1.66 days per month**, totaling **20 days per year**. Accrual follows this monthly rate for this tenure band.

## Paid Sick Leave Allocation
Employees receive **5 days** of paid sick leave annually. Sick leave is granted upfront on **January 1st**.

## PTO Carryover Limit
Employees can carry over a maximum of **5 days** of PTO into the next calendar year. Carryover is capped at this amount.

## Q4 Feature Freeze Time-Off Blackout
Engineering and Support cannot take more than **2 consecutive days off** during the “Q4 Feature Freeze.” The blackout period runs from **Dec 15 – Jan 5**.
