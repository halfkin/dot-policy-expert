# Employee Benefits Policy (Internal)

## Health Insurance Premium Coverage
Loomo provides **100% premium coverage** for employees. Loomo provides **50% premium coverage** for dependents via **Blue Shield**.

## 401(k) Match and Vesting
Loomo matches **100%** of employee contributions up to **4% of base salary**. The match follows a **2-year vesting schedule**.

## Work-From-Home Setup Credit
Employees receive a **$1,200** one-time Work-From-Home setup credit. This credit is intended for initial setup costs.

## Professional Development Budget
Employees receive a **$2,000** annual professional development budget. The budget renews annually.

## Remote-First Sync Hours
Loomo is “Remote-First.” Employees must be online during core “Sync Hours” from **10 AM – 2 PM CST**.

## Recruitment Benefits Summary PTO Rollover Statement
The benefits summary distributed during recruitment states employees may roll over “up to one week” of PTO. This statement is presented as part of the recruitment benefits summary.
